# Things to tell the owner

Points to pass on with the season update. Add to this list rather than relying on chat history.

- **Test copies.** The owner-private test page (a claude.ai page) and the separate Sites preview are not linked from this repository. The Sites preview can only be redeployed by Codex from the branch; see [`docs/PRIVATE-PREVIEW.md`](../../PRIVATE-PREVIEW.md).
- **Spotlight on the test page** rolls locally and gets no serial number; real numbered pulls need the live card registry.
- **Data rights.** NBA/NFL Ultimate Team benchmarks use publisher ratings from NBA 2K and Madden as references, and soccer uses facts derived from Transfermarkt-based open datasets with the game's own ratings. Sources are listed in [`docs/CREDITS.md`](../../CREDITS.md). Check terms before any commercial launch.
- **Online pairing splits until both phones update.** Any gameplay or rating change bumps the online queues, so an updated phone and an old one won't be matched until both reload.
- **Spotlight/GOAT stay above 99** as server-registered ultra-rares; they were deliberately left out of the new FC-style scale so owned numbered cards keep their value.
- **New Era 65,000 coins** is honoured as asked, even though it is large against the game's 60/30-per-match economy.
- **Older builds reject a cloud save that contains soccer** until they update.
