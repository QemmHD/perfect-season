# EA FC 27 Ultimate Team research (2026-11-01)

Condensed findings from a research pass over public FC 26/27 coverage (patch notes, EA deep dives, community trackers). The individual source links were in the research report and are not reproduced here; the parts that shipped cite their sources in [`docs/UT-CONTENT-2.md`](../../UT-CONTENT-2.md) and [`docs/RATINGS-AUDIT.md`](../../RATINGS-AUDIT.md). "Translation for us" at the end is what the game adopted.

## Launch baseline (FC 27)
- Best base golds: 91 (Mbappé, Haaland, Putellas); ~14 base cards at 90+ out of 21,000+ players (~0.07%).
- Base Icons start at 88; top Pelé/Maradona 95, R9/Zidane 94. New Icons debut 85–86 → auto-upgrade to 89–91 ("Champion") on a known date. Heroes 85–89 (most 86–87). Hall of FUT 84–85 bought with Gallery tokens.
- Starter: pick a league pair; Foundations objectives give an untradeable ~83 "Squad Foundations" player (~5 OVR above starter pool).
- Best possible launch squad (FC 26): team rating 90.
- Large Gold Pack per-item odds: 75–82 94.3%, 83 3.38%, 84 1.34%, 85 0.43%, 86 0.23%, 87 0.16%, 88 0.16%, 89 0.036%, 90 0.012% → each step above 83 ≈2.5× rarer (copy this pyramid).
- Tiers: Bronze 45–64, Silver 65–74, Gold 75+ (FC 27 removed common/rare). Holographic variants: same stats, rarer, more Item Score (Regular ≤87, Pristine 88+).
- Walkout at 86+: stadium shot → nation flag → league badge → club badge; promo = stage colour + stairs; bronze/silver skip animation; double-walkout hint; player-steerable flourish.
- Item Score (value curve, drives Streamlined SBCs & Gallery): Bronze 20, Silver 35, 75:90, 80:180, 83:410, 84:830, 85:2100, 86:4100, 88:8300, 90:14000, 92:20000, 95:40000, 99:100000; TOTW +25%, Hero +25%, Icon +50%, Holo higher.

## Promo ladder (FC 26 full year; FC 27 so far similar)
Sep launch/TOTW 80–92 · Oct OTW floor 83 / Scream 84–90 / Future Stars 85–90 / Destined 85–91 · Nov Black Friday 85–92 (Icons to 96) · Dec Winter Wildcards 85–91 (Icons 86–95) · Jan TOTY 94–97 (no 99s; TOTY Icons 91–95) · Feb Future Stars 86–91 · Feb–Mar Evo caps 88–90 · Mar Birthday/RTTF 89–96 · Apr–May TOTS 90–97 · Jun–Aug FUTTIES weekly 99s (FC 26: 60 cards at 99 all year).
FC 27 design: slower weak→strong, faster strong→elite; end-game boosts 20% smaller; fewer campaign weeks; PlayStyles+ capped at 3; EA admitted FC 26 Evos too strong — keep Evo ceiling below top promos.
Typical engaged squad by month (estimate): Sep 80–83 · late Sep 83–85 · Oct 85–87 · Nov 87–89 · Dec 88–90 · Jan 90–92 · Feb–Mar 91–93 · Apr–May 93–95 · summer 95–97.

## Pricing (FC 27)
Large Bronze 750 (12) · Large Silver 3,750 (12; EV 61%) · Large Gold 7,500 (12; 84+ 8.8%; EV 47%) · Triple T 35k (6; 84+ 73%) · Super Sub 40k (6; 85+ 43%) · promo packs 55–95k (15–20 items, 85/86+ 46–81%) · Large Gold Players 60k (12; EV 29%) · Heroic 250k. Coin/FCP ratio 50–80 at launch → 140–200 by Jan (coin inflation). Lightning Rounds: limited global stock + timer. Store tabs: My Packs, Browse, Previewed, Token Store. Preview Packs (see contents first; cooldown). Draft entry 15k coins or token. No min-rare guarantees in FC 27.
Earnings: match ~500–600 (FC 26; FC 27 "several times" more); SP 15–100/match capped 850/day; daily 3 objectives +1,000 coins; 5-day login calendar (D1 75+ pack + small silver, D2 consumables, D3 1,000 SP, D4 78+ pick 1 of 2, D5 coin boost); Rivals weekly 3/1/0 points, 15=base 30=upgrade (Div 10: 3×81+; Div 1: 95k + packs); Squad Battles weekly (Gold 3: 5k + 8 gold + 2×81+; Elite 1: 25k + 15×77+ + 3×84+); Champions 15 matches → tokens + coins (Rank 1: 250k + 450 tokens; token store e.g. 25 tokens = 5×82+, 200 = 3×88+). Quick-sell: Bronze 28–48, Silver 228–259, Gold 533–646 by OVR; quick-sell recovery 5 per 31 days within 7 days.
Weekly haul mid-level ≈ 30–60k coins + 10–15 packs ≈ 1 promo pack or 5–8 Large Golds. Pack EV target 45–60% of price; rest recovered via Item Score.
Compensation practice: grant correct reward and let players keep mistaken items; season pass resets each season, items stay.

## Systems
Season Pass 30 levels/~35-day season, ~30k SP, daily SP cap. Streamlined SBCs: points target (Item Score), duplicates count, partial progress saved; players want a favourite/LOCK flag. Gallery: 100+ themed sets; cards count forever even after sold; grades D–S; levels with milestones every 5; tokens buy kits/Hall of FUT. Evolutions: caps (max OVR/pace/PlayStyles/position), branching Pathways, Chain Preview. PlayStyles Lab. Chemistry 0–3/player, team 33 (club 2/4/7, nation 2/5/8, league 3/5/8). Live Events (single-player chained stages, difficulty ladder). Draft 4 matches knockout. Rush 5v5. Campaign Hub (current campaign objectives/SBCs/Evos/events/next reward in one screen). Foundations onboarding.

## Translation for us (decided)
- Scale: Bronze 40–64, Silver 65–74, Gold 75–99; walkout 86+.
- Base season pool pyramid: common gold 75–82, good 83–86, elite 87–90, ~10–15 cards per sport at 90+ with top 91 (best MVP seasons). Steps above 83 ≈2.5× rarer each.
- Legends: base 88–95 (only 2–4 at 94–95); promo legends up to 96.
- Promos: Weekly Standouts 80–92 (TOTW), Landmark ~85–93, Hot Season (TOTY-like) 94–97 (no 99s), a late "finale" tier with sparse 98–99 (~1% of specials).
- Spotlight/GOAT: KEEP above-99 as server-registered ultra-rares (owned numbered cards; don't devalue). Note to owner.
- Economy: keep our coin scale (60/30 per match, gold 900) but match FC ratios: pack EV 45–60% of price; quick-sell/Item Score curve doubling per OVR above 83.
- Copy list: Item Score streamlined SBCs + LOCK/favourite flag; Gallery counts forever; live-upgrade promos (+1 per milestone from Club Season/own results); debut→champion legends; odds pyramid screen; walkout sequence (team city → conference/league → club) at 86+; holographic variants; Evolutions caps/chains (ceiling below top promos); 30-level pass + daily XP cap; Live Events ladder; tiered weekly rewards; daily 3 objectives + 5-day login calendar; Campaign Hub; Foundations onboarding + Preview Pack with cooldown.
