## Headline numbers

- **FC 27 scale:** Bronze 40–64, Silver 65–74, Gold 75–99; base cards top out at 91; legends 88–95; Hot Season (TOTY-like) 94–97; walkout at 86+; Item Score curve doubling per overall above 83.
- **Content calendar:** 12 seasons from 2026-10-26 to 2027-11-21, then a 364-day repeat. Weekly rhythm: Monday reset, Wednesday Weekly Standouts, Friday program drop, weekend flash windows. 98–99 cards only in the Finale (about 0.8% of specials).
