PERFECT SEASON · V2 MOCK-UPS

Companion to GAME-DESIGN-V2.md. Everything here is original concept art drawn in SVG and CSS: no team, league or publisher marks. Real player names appear only as labels for the archetypes. Phone frames are 390×844 shown at 80%. These are layout and style targets, not screenshots of a build.

1 · In-match HUD and controls

One control grammar for three sports: a floating stick under the left thumb (push past the ring to sprint), and at most four right-thumb targets. A is always the timed action and never moves. The controlled player, the target and the gold ring always sit inside the action-safe box above the controls.

2 · Character lineup: "Broadcast Pixel"

Five-heads-tall athletes with a hard #14121c outline and three tones per material. Identity is carried, in order, by build, hair silhouette, hair colour, head gear, skin tone, facial hair and asymmetric gear. Each archetype is shown at match size (1.5 px per art pixel) and at 4× zoom. All of it is generated from one small appearance record per player, the way the in-game code-drawn rig would do it. No logos, tattoos or brand marks.

3 · MINUTES: time-played tracks and the board

Active minutes (capped at 90 a day) fill three ladders: today, this week and career. The same capped minutes drive a weekly league with promotion and relegation. Sized to the real economy: an hour a day earns a Silver Pack a day and a Gold Pack a week, and time never pays more coins than the matches do.
